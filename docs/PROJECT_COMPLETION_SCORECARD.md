## Meaningful evidence already in place

- The protected Next.js/Prisma/PostgreSQL Headquarters and dedicated Docker
  Compose service separation have been tested in CI, not on an approved live host.
- One explicitly requested permitted-site Playwright website visit can generate
  evidence, and owner-entered product reference matching, cautious scoring,
  owner review, manually sent quotes, and owner-reported conversions use
  persisted states. This does **not** autonomously discover customers at scale.
- The actual React Three Fiber room/desk scene and 2D/mobile mode are verified
  in software Chromium WebGL; no real owner GPU FPS benchmark exists.
- Local FFmpeg produces a genuine 20-second vertical MP4 from an uploaded
  owner-photo-type source, and the owner can approve/hand it off manually.
  CI images and results are synthetic fixtures, not owner products or posts.
- **14 out of 16 named roles (87.5%) have some local report/workflow capability**.
  The remaining two platform-dependent roles (Instagram prospect discovery
  and compliant phone calling) are unconfigured. This is **NOT** 87.5% project
  completion: most existing desks are deterministic, evidence-driven scripts,
  not independent self-directed AI workers or live connected channels.
- The owner-reported inbound Customer Inbox can store manual, actual inbound
  enquiries and manual replies, with local category suggestions. It is not a
  live WhatsApp/Instagram connected account, and no outbound message is sent.
- Owner-opted-in India-local daily briefing can evaluate 16 role states while
  blocked channels remain explicitly blocked. No model API charge is incurred;
  local compute, VPS and electricity are not metered.

## Critical acceptance gates remaining before calling the HQ complete

1. Independently configured HQ host/domain/HTTPS, real network egress
   containment, non-root Chromium sandbox/security, monitoring, and production
   rollback/restore operations. Never deploy to the SPLCART store.
2. Genuine licensed owner jewellery photographs, realistic style approval,
   measured target-device 3D responsiveness, real video review/retention,
   permitted social handoff and sustainable local GPU/CPU cost measurement.
3. Real legally usable wholesale leads from independent permitted business
   sources, documented owner consent and outreach decisions, live lead-to-quote
   results, and appropriately verified revenue if claimed.
4. Compliant external Instagram/YouTube/inbound/WhatsApp/telephone workflows
   only where a real permitted account, source rights and consent exist. No
   automated cold Instagram DMs and no unlawful promotional robocalls.
5. Meaningful bounded multi-step autonomous decisions/actions with durable
   checkpoints for each genuinely operational role, rather than only daily
   evidence snapshots and deterministic summaries.
6. Owner-requested privacy erasure and closed-case retention controls, backup
   expiry/offsite deletion procedure, plus legal/privacy review before using
   real customer records.

Do not raise the percentage merely because a unit test was added or a role
name was displayed. Raise it for tested improvements in actual business
capabilities and independently verified operational readiness. Do not invent
